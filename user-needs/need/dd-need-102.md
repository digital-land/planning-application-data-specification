name: Record when an authority has resolved to refuse
statement: >
  As a planning authority, I need to record when the determining authority has
  resolved to refuse permission before the formal decision notice is issued so
  that the application status reflects the expected direction of the case while
  refusal reasons are finalised or another body considers whether to intervene.
actors:
  - planning-authority
scope: in
themes:
  - processing
  - transparency
source:
  - type: interview
    notes: Camden explained that a resolution to refuse can occur where a committee overturns an officer recommendation, refusal reasons still need to be formulated or agreed, or another body is considering whether to call in the application.
variations:
  - dd-need-111
next_step:
notes: |
  This need is about the interim position after a determining authority has resolved to refuse permission, but before the formal decision notice is issued. It is separate from the final decision date.

  Related needs: dd-need-014, dd-need-059, dd-need-079.
