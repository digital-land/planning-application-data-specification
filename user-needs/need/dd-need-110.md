name: Know when an authority has resolved to grant

  As a planning-system user, I need to know when the determining authority has
  resolved to grant permission before the formal decision notice is issued so

  and is awaiting legal agreement, call-in consideration or other finalisation.

      after a resolution to grant while legal agreements are finalised, and that
      this is often unclear on public registers.
