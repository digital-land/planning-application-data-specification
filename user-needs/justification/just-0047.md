The `time-extension-agreed` value lets the planning permission timeline record whether and when an extension of time was agreed.

This satisfies the first part of the need. The specification does not record the revised determination deadline agreed through the extension, which cannot be reliably inferred from the event date. A service would need that agreed deadline as data before it could display the revised timetable or assess progress against it.
