The `demolition-con-area` value in `application-types` identifies applications
for planning permission for relevant demolition in a conservation area. In the
structured submission data, `description` records what is proposed and `reason`
records why the demolition is considered necessary.

Linking a decision notice to the planning application and recording `decision`
and `decision-date` provides the outcome and when it was made. Together, the
submission data and planning authority decision record let practitioners
understand the proposal, its justification and its outcome.
