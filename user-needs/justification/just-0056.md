The `lbc` value in `application-types` identifies listed building consent applications. The structured submission records whether alteration or demolition is proposed, the types of alteration and the listed building grade. Supporting documents referenced from the submission provide the detailed proposal available to the planning practitioner.

Linking a decision notice to the planning application and recording `decision` and `decision-date` lets practitioners understand the outcome of the proposal and when the decision was made. Together, the submitted proposal and recorded decision support assessment and stewardship of the listed building.
