The `invalid-from` value lets the planning permission timeline record whether and when the authority treated an application as invalid.

Recording the invalid event with `event-date` and `planning-application` lets users understand the application's status during validation and compare that point with submission, receipt, later validation activity or other process events.

Reasons for invalidity and details of missing information are covered by the related, more specific user needs rather than this status-and-timing need.
