The `valid-from` timeline event records when the authority treated an application
as valid for statutory or process purposes. The `time-extension-agreed` event
records when an extension of time was agreed, while `decision-date` records when
the decision was made.

Together with `application-types` and `development-scale`, these fields let users
calculate elapsed determination times and compare them across broad application
routes and major or minor development scale.

This partially satisfies need-ps-025 because the model does not record the
applicable determination deadline or a revised deadline agreed through an
extension of time, Planning Performance Agreement or EIA timetable. Without that
deadline, a service manager cannot determine from the data alone whether an
application met the timetable that applied to it.

It partially satisfies need-ps-026 because the model does not yet provide the
full development-type reporting categories needed to monitor determination times
across all PS1 and PS2 development types.

It partially satisfies need-ps-030 for the same reason: without those reporting
categories, a policy user cannot consistently identify whether delays or capacity
challenges are concentrated in particular development types.
