The `planning-officer-recommendation` field records the formal recommendation to grant or refuse made by the planning officer.

This partially satisfies the need because it provides the recommendation but not the authority's assessment or the reasons supporting it. A service would need access to further information, such as an officer, delegated or equivalent decision report, to present the wider assessment and reasoning.
