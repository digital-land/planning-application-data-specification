The `withdrawn-date` field lets the planning application record show when an application was withdrawn.

Withdrawal is also a core fact about the application: recording
`withdrawn-date` signifies that the application was withdrawn and the date it
ceased progressing towards determination. This lets planning service managers
distinguish withdrawn applications from the active caseload and account for
them consistently in outcome and workload reporting.
