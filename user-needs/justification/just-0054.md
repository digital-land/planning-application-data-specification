The `hh` value in `application-types` identifies householder planning applications. The related `site` locates each application and `description` records the proposed development.

Linking a decision notice to the planning application and recording `decision` and `decision-date` lets users see the outcome of householder applications and when they were decided. Together, these fields support understanding previous changes when assessing current proposals and monitoring patterns in incremental neighbourhood change.
