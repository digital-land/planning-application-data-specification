Linking each condition applied to a decision to a referenced
`planning-condition` record supports consistent identification and reuse of
condition wording.

This only partially satisfies the need because the model does not explicitly
distinguish a standard or reusable clause from wording drafted for one decision.
The condition text and reason design decision also records as unresolved whether
`planning-condition` represents a reusable clause or the exact condition as
imposed in a particular case.
