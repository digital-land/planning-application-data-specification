The `major` value in `development-scale` identifies applications classified by the local planning authority as major development. Linking planning applications to decision notices lets users understand the volume and outcomes of those applications.

This only partially satisfies the need because these fields do not identify which applications concern public service infrastructure or the type of service involved. Users cannot therefore isolate outcomes for health, education, justice or community infrastructure.
