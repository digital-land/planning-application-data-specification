The `received-date` field establishes when an application entered the authority's
caseload. Validation timeline events show when it was treated as valid or invalid,
while `withdrawn-date` and `decision-date` identify two ways in which it can cease
to await a decision.

Where complete historical records are available, these dates can be used to
reconstruct a candidate set of applications awaiting a decision at a particular
date. This partially supports operational workload monitoring in need-ps-001 and
statistical reporting in need-ps-028.

The needs are not fully satisfied because the agreed statistical definition of
an application awaiting a decision has not yet been confirmed, including the
treatment of invalid applications and other closed states. The specification
also does not by itself ensure that records predating its adoption are available
for a complete historical series.
