The `lbc` value in `application-types` identifies listed building consent
applications. The structured submission records whether demolition is proposed,
whether it is partial or total, what is to be demolished, the reason for the
demolition and the listed building grade.

Together, these fields let a planning practitioner identify the proposed loss
and assess its effect on the listed building's heritage significance. Supporting
documents referenced from the submission provide the detailed evidence needed
to determine whether the demolition is justified.
