The `resolution-to-grant` and `resolution-to-refuse` events record what the determining authority resolved and when.

This partially satisfies the need because the events record the direction of the resolution but not what the committee considered or the reasons for reaching it. A service would need access to further information, such as a committee report, minutes, recording or another meeting record, to present the consideration and reasoning.
