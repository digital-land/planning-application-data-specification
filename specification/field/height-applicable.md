Is a height applicable to the proposed development?

For agricultural or forestry private roads and agricultural excavation or waste deposition, the section asks:

What is the height of the proposed development? ___ metres   Not applicable

A supplied height corresponds to Yes. 
The printed Not applicable answer corresponds to No. 
An unanswered question remains unanswered.

- [Private road for agricultural or forestry use, form 017, ECAB 2019.1 RP](https://ecab.planningportal.co.uk/uploads/appPDF/E2734Form017_england_en.pdf).
