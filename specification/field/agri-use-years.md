---
field: agri-use-years
name: Agricultural use years
description: The years part of the length of time the land on which the proposed development would be located has been used for agriculture for the purposes of a trade or business.
datatype: integer
cardinality: 1
entry-date: 2026-11-20
end-date: ''
notes: ''
---

Example usage:

How long has the land on which the proposed development would be located been in use for agriculture for the purposes of a trade or business?

Years: ___ Months: ___

This field records the Years answer. The Months answer is recorded in `agri-use-months`.
