## Class MA: Commercial, business and service use to dwellinghouses

For prior approval applications for a change of use from commercial, business and service use to dwellinghouses (Class MA), answer Yes if any of the following restrictions apply. The question combines restrictions affecting the building and its land with restrictions affecting the area where the building is located.

The first group concerns land covered by the building or within its curtilage:

- A site of special scientific interest.
- A listed building or land within its curtilage.
- A scheduled monument or land within its curtilage.
- A safety hazard area.
- A military explosives storage area.

The second group concerns whether the building is in:

- An area of outstanding natural beauty.
- An area specified by the Secretary of State for the enhancement and protection of the natural beauty and amenity of the countryside.
- The Broads.
- A National Park.
- A World Heritage Site.

Answer No if none of these restrictions apply. A single answer is recorded; the field does not identify which individual restriction applies.

These criteria are taken from section 4a of the [Class MA application form, version ECAB 2024.1](https://ecab.planningportal.co.uk/uploads/appPDF/E2734Form064_england_en.pdf).


## Class Q: Agricultural buildings to dwellinghouses

For prior approval applications for a change of use from agricultural buildings to dwellinghouses (Class Q), the scope is any part of the land, site or building. This route includes buildings on former agricultural units. Use the following question and list instead of the Class MA criteria above:

Is any part of the land, site or building:

- in a conservation area;
- in an area of outstanding natural beauty;
- in an area specified by the Secretary of State for the purposes of enhancement and protection of the natural beauty and amenity of the countryside;
- in the Broads;
- in a National Park;
- in a World Heritage Site;
- in a site of special scientific interest;
- in a safety hazard area;
- in a military explosives storage area;
- a scheduled monument (or the site contains one);
- a listed building (or within the curtilage of a listed building).

Answer Yes if any listed restriction applies, otherwise No.

Source: section 4a of the [Class Q application form, version ECAB 2024.2](https://ecab.planningportal.co.uk/uploads/appPDF/E2734Form044_england_en.pdf).
