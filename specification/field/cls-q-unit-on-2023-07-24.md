## Current established agricultural unit

Was the site part of the established agricultural unit on 24 July 2023?

Answer Yes or No.

Source: section 4b of the [prior approval application form for agricultural buildings to dwellinghouses (Class Q), version ECAB 2024.2](https://ecab.planningportal.co.uk/uploads/appPDF/E2734Form044_england_en.pdf).

## Former established agricultural unit

Where the site is no longer part of an established agricultural unit, was it still part of one on 24 July 2023?

Answer Yes or No.

Source: the former established agricultural unit section on page 3 of the same form. Its navigation calls this section 4c, although its printed heading repeats 4b.
