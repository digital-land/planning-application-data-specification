If yes, have all the parties to all of the terminated agricultural tenancy agreements agreed that the site is no longer required for agricultural use?

Answer Yes or No.

This question follows a Yes answer to whether agricultural tenancy agreements were terminated in the year before development is proposed to begin for the purpose of carrying out the proposed change of use. It records agreement about agricultural need, not agreement to terminate a tenancy.

Source: section 5 of the [prior approval application form for agricultural buildings to dwellinghouses (Class Q), version ECAB 2024.2](https://ecab.planningportal.co.uk/uploads/appPDF/E2734Form044_england_en.pdf).
