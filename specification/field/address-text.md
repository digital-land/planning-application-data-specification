A text representation of an address or site.

In a `contact-address`, this must provide the information needed for postal
delivery, such as a building number or name, street, locality and town or city.

In a `site-address`, this may be a postal address, site name or a readable
description of unaddressed land. It helps people identify the site but does not
define its extent; the site boundary is authoritative for that purpose.
