## Agricultural buildings to dwellinghouses (Class Q)

Please provide details of any flooding risks and how these will be mitigated.

A flood risk assessment should accompany the application where the site:

- is in Flood Zones 2 or 3 (Check online: https://flood-map-for-planning.service.gov.uk/ ); or
- is in an area with critical drainage problems (Check with the Local Planning Authority, it will have been notified of such areas by the Environment Agency).

Source: section 7 of the [prior approval application form for agricultural buildings to dwellinghouses (Class Q), version ECAB 2024.2](https://ecab.planningportal.co.uk/uploads/appPDF/E2734Form044_england_en.pdf).
