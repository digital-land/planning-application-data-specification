An optional array of 8-digit Unique Street Reference Numbers (USRNs). Each value
identifies a street associated with the site, for example a street used to describe
or access it. A service should provide a value only where it is known from an
authoritative street-gazetteer source. The planning authority may validate it
against its Local Street Gazetteer or the National Street Gazetteer.
