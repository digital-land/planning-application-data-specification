name: Roof PV exceeds projection limit
description: Whether any proposed solar photovoltaic equipment exceeds the applicable projection or height limit for the roof.
datatype: boolean
cardinality: 1
entry-date: 2026-11-26
end-date: ''

Where the solar photovoltaic equipment is to be installed:

- On a pitched roof, would any part of the solar photovoltaic equipment protrude more that 0.2 metres beyond the plane of the roof slope (when measured from the perpendicular with the external surface of the roof slope); or
- On a flat roof, would any part of the solar photovoltaic equipment be higher than 1 metre above the highest part of the roof (excluding any chimney).

Yes / No

If the answer to the question above is Yes, the source form states that the proposal will exceed the limits set by legislation. Applicants should not continue with this application and should seek advice from the local planning authority on the best course of action.

Source: section 4 of the [prior approval form for roof-mounted solar PV on non-domestic buildings, version ECAB 2023](https://ecab.planningportal.co.uk/uploads/appPDF/E2734Form052_england_en.pdf).
