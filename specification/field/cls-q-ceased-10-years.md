If yes, the site was part of an established agricultural unit on 24 July 2023 and subsequently ceased to be, will it have been at least 10 years since the site was part of an established agricultural unit prior to the date development begins?

Answer Yes, No or Not applicable, recorded as `yes`, `no` or `not-applicable` respectively.

Source: the former established agricultural unit section on page 3 of the [prior approval application form for agricultural buildings to dwellinghouses (Class Q), version ECAB 2024.2](https://ecab.planningportal.co.uk/uploads/appPDF/E2734Form044_england_en.pdf). The form's navigation calls this section 4c, although its printed heading repeats 4b.
