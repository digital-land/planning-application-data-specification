name: Consent of all tenancy parties
notes: Required when agricultural tenancy agreements exist. Consent must cover all parties to all relevant agreements, not only the tenants.
---

This field records consent to the proposed change of use from all parties to the relevant agricultural tenancy agreements. Notifying a party of the proposal does not establish their consent.

Answer Yes only when all parties have consented. Answer No if any party has not consented.

Source: [Class MA application form, section 5, version ECAB 2024.1](https://ecab.planningportal.co.uk/uploads/appPDF/E2734Form064_england_en.pdf).
