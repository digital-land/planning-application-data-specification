Would the total electrical generation capacity of all the equipment installed on the building under permitted development rights (previously and in this proposal) be no more than 50 kilowatts?

Yes / No

If the answer to the question above is Yes, the source form states that the proposal will not exceed the 50 kilowatt ‘microgeneration’ threshold that would require a prior approval application. Applicants should not continue with this application, but should ensure all other eligibility criteria for the permitted development right are met before starting work.

Source: section 4 of the [prior approval form for roof-mounted solar PV on non-domestic buildings, version ECAB 2023](https://ecab.planningportal.co.uk/uploads/appPDF/E2734Form052_england_en.pdf).
